Advanced
Defaults were measured on the live cable: tone 10 s, OTDR 3 s, about 13 s a fibre. A longer tone does not slow the run until it passes about 11 s, and it covers slow responses from FMS. OTDR 3 s makes a wrong guess cost about 18 s; 1 s was erratic (14 to 35 s). Tone is CW (0 Hz) at 1550 nm.
Smart search is on. If a fibre is not where it should be, the run checks the fibres either side, the ribbon reversed, the ribbons and bundles next to it, then sweeps the ribbon. Once it proves a pattern (for example two ribbons crossed) it tries that first for the next fibres, and every cross is retested before it is reported.
